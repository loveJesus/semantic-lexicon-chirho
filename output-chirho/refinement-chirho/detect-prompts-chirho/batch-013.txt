# Sense Refinement Detection — Batch 013

You are a biblical lexicographer reviewing sense entries for a Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek lexicon.
Each entry below is a single "sense" that was produced by clustering translation signatures 
and then merging with an LLM. Some senses may have been OVER-LUMPED — they contain genuinely 
distinct meanings that should be separate senses.

## Your Task

For each sense, determine if it should be SPLIT into multiple senses. Look for:
- Literal vs. figurative/metaphorical uses lumped together
- Concrete vs. abstract meanings combined
- Multiple distinct referents (e.g., "father" = biological parent + patron/advisor + originator)
- Physical vs. spiritual/theological uses merged
- Different syntactic frames producing different meanings

## Output Format

Return a JSON array. For senses that need NO splitting, omit them entirely.
For senses that SHOULD be split, include an entry like:

```json
[
  {
    "senseIdChirho": 79287,
    "lemmaIdChirho": "H0001",
    "currentLabelChirho": "father, immediate male parent",
    "splitProposalChirho": [
      {
        "newLabelChirho": "biological/legal father",
        "criteriaChirho": "Direct parent-child relationship, possessive constructions with specific person"
      },
      {
        "newLabelChirho": "metaphorical father (patron, advisor, protector)",
        "criteriaChirho": "Title of respect for prophets, advisors, or authority figures; 'father to Pharaoh' type usage"
      }
    ],
    "confidenceChirho": "high",
    "reasoningChirho": "Description explicitly mentions 'title of respect or metaphorical originator' alongside biological father"
  }
]
```

Only propose splits where you have MEDIUM or HIGH confidence. Do NOT split function words (articles, 
prepositions, particles, conjunctions) unless there is a genuinely distinct semantic sense (not just 
syntactic variation). Proper nouns generally should NOT be split.

If NO senses in this batch need splitting, return an empty array: []

### H0123 / Sense 80027 (100 occ, sense 1 of 1)
**Label:** Edom (person and nation)
**Lemma gloss:** Edom — Esau's byname meaning 'red,' and by extension the nation, territory, and people descended from him southeast of the Dead Sea
**Description:** Proper name with dual reference: (1) The personal name of Esau, Jacob's twin brother, given the byname 'Edom' (meaning 'red') after selling his birthright for red stew (Gen 25:30; 36:1, 8). (2) The nation and territory descended from Esau, located south of the Dead Sea in the mountainous region of Seir (Gen 36:9; Num 20:14-21). Edom features prominently in prophetic oracles of judgment (Isa 34:5-6; Jer 49:7-22; Ezek 25:12-14; Obad 1:1; Amos 1:11) and in historical narratives of conflict with Israel (1 Sam 14:47; 2 Sam 8:14; 1 Kgs 11:14-16). **Enriched:** The proper name Edom, from the root '-d-m ('red'), given to Esau after he traded his birthright for red lentil stew (Gen 25:30). The name extends to Esau's descendants as a people (Num 20:18-21; 1 Sam 14:47), their mountain territory southeast of the Dead Sea (Gen 32:3; 36:9), and the king who represents them (Num 20:18). In prophetic discourse Edom becomes emblematic of opposition to Israel, subject to oracles of judgment in Obadiah, Jeremiah 49, and Ezekiel 25. Spanish, French, and German all transliterate as 'Edom,' while the Septuagint renders it Edom or Idoumaia for the region.

**Label:** because, since, inasmuch as
**Lemma gloss:** Because, since, inasmuch as — a causal conjunction introducing the ground or reason for a statement, especially in prophetic judgment oracles
**Description:** The causal conjunction/adverb yaʿan (יַעַן) meaning 'because, since, inasmuch as,' introducing the reason or cause for a statement, typically in prophetic judgment oracles (e.g., Lev 26:43; Num 11:20; Isa 3:16; Jer 29:19; Ezek 5:11, 13:10, 25:3, 28:2,6,17, 34:8,21, 36:3,13; Zeph 3:11). **Enriched:** A causal conjunction meaning 'because, since, inasmuch as,' typically introducing the reason or charge in prophetic judgment oracles.
